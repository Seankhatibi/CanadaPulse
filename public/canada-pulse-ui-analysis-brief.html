Strong

Clear emotional hook: same income, different province. This is a real shareable premise.

Risk

Income and province are URL-driven. A visible income slider/input would make the page feel more usable and viral.

Opportunity

Add a one-click share card: “On $92k, my province ranks #X and pays $Y.”

Trust

Methodology and source caveats should be visible near the calculator, not only in separate pages.

UI Review Prompts For Your AI Agent

  • Does the first viewport make the tax-dollar idea instantly obvious?
  • Can a mobile user change province and income without reading instructions?
  • Which specific element would someone screenshot and share?
  • Are modeled/demo estimates labeled clearly enough to preserve trust?
  • What should be removed, moved, or enlarged for Canadian youth using the app on phone?
